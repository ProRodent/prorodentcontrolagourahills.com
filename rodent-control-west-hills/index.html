Early signs worth acting on.

A quick scan of your home against the points below can sharpen what you tell us on the call.

  • A thump in the ceiling around dusk or dawn.
  • Droppings in a kitchen drawer.
  • A sprinkler or drip line with chewed tubing.
  • A faint squeak that seems to come from within a wall.

Yards in the San Fernando Valley.

Protective steps for rodent mess.

This is not medical advice. It is a short list of precautions that public health pages suggest for homes showing rodent signs.

  • A shop vacuum on a dusty pile is a bad idea; a leaf blower is worse.
  • Make a draft with two openings, then busy yourself elsewhere until the time is up.
  • Gloves go on first, come off last and end up in the trash bag.
  • Wet every inch you plan to touch, including the spot underneath the pellets.
  • Keep pets away from the sealed bag until it is in the bin.
  • Scrub under the fingernails and up the wrists, then use a fresh towel.
