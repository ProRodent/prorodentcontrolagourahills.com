Protective steps for rodent mess.

Cleaning feels urgent, yet hurrying is the wrong move. The steps here keep dust and germs down and come from federal guidance.

  • A shop vacuum on a dusty pile is a bad idea; a leaf blower is worse.
  • Open every window you can reach, then step outside for a half hour.
  • Choose gloves long enough to cover the wrist, where dust likes to land.
  • Wet every inch you plan to touch, including the spot underneath the pellets.
  • Knot the bag firmly and put it in an outdoor bin with a lid.
  • Fever or aches in the weeks after contact are worth mentioning to a doctor, along with the rodent exposure.
