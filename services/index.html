Putting the stages in sequence.

A single order covers most jobs. It opens with the inspection, which names the animal. Next trapping clears whatever is inside, then sealing shuts the doors against newcomers. Cleanup or repair closes the job when the house needs it.

You need not buy every stage. Perhaps all you want is a new vent cover or a traced smell; tell us and the estimate lists just that.

Questions about the list.

Why is there no emergency page?

That was a choice. Urgent help is described on each page, someone answers at all hours and a same-day visit leaves whenever the schedule has room.

Which job do I start with?

If unsure, book the inspection. It names the animal and sets out the jobs in order, each with a price.

Can I order just one piece?

Yes. Any single job can be booked alone, although sealing before repairs saves you from doing the repair twice.

Are these services for businesses as well?

Yes, and the commercial page explains receiving doors, shared walls, dumpster use and the short note we leave behind.
