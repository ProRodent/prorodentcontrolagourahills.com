Getting onto this list took two checks. First, the place needed an entry in the network's coverage file. Second, an independent ZIP lookup had to put it inside fifteen miles of Agoura Hills in a straight line.

Each figure is rounded to a tenth of a mile and runs to the heart of the ZIP area, so a house near the boundary could sit a mile nearer or farther. Use the figures as a rough guide, and ask on the phone if exact distance matters. Whoever answers can judge a borderline street better than a table can.

Whenever the schedule has room, a technician leaving Agoura Hills can generally reach any listed place that day. ZIP and distance FAQ.

Why isn't Agoura Hills in the table?

The table covers nearby places that need their own pages. Agoura Hills sits at the center of the map, and its ZIP codes, 91301 and 91376, are covered. Street addresses in the city get confirmed on the call.

I live at the edge of a listed town. Is that a problem?

No. Every ZIP code listed is covered. Mileage is taken from the center of a ZIP area, so fringe addresses get sorted out on the phone.

Two places, one name. Which is meant?

Check the type column. Westlake Village is its own city, for example, while Newbury Park is a community inside Thousand Oaks.

Is the Ladyface Trail address an office?

No. It is there so directories can list us. Nobody is stationed at it, and each visit takes place at the customer's building.
