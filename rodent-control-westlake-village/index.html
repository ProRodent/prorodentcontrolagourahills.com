After you call.

The order never varies, and there is a reason. Removing animals before closing gaps traps them inside, and sealing before checking leaves new animals unaccounted for. The recheck confirms the result. Questions about any step are welcome on the call.

The Conejo Valley setting.

Fences and block walls act as highways. A rat can follow the top of a fence to a roof edge without touching the ground, so fence tops are part of the survey.

Tile or composition roofs have gaps where they meet the wall or the vent. A technician checks those joints, especially where flashing has lifted or a vent screen is bent.
