A wet-wipe routine for pellets and nests.

Cleaning feels urgent, yet hurrying is the wrong move. The steps here keep dust and germs down and come from federal guidance.

  • Feather dusters flick old droppings into the air, so keep them away too.
  • Plan on half an hour of airing before any wiping begins.
  • Skin picks up what gloves block, so wear them before reaching for stained items.
  • The disinfectant must sit before it works, so do not wipe at once.
  • Seal the bag before washing your hands.
  • Washing is the final step, whether or not you wore gloves.
