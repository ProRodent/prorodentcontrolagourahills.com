Why the phone rings.

These are the signs that make people pick up the phone.

  • Smudges on an attic beam where fur has rubbed.
  • Insulation that looks flattened in tracks.
  • A cabinet under the sink with a gnawed hole around the pipe.
  • Pellets on top of the water heater.

Read this before you clean.

This is not medical advice. It is a short list of precautions that public health pages suggest for homes showing rodent signs.

  • A shop vacuum on a dusty pile is a bad idea; a leaf blower is worse.
  • Open every window you can reach, then step outside for a half hour.
  • Slip on rubber, latex or vinyl gloves before handling anything soiled, and rinse hands afterward.
  • Federal guidance suggests a bleach mix of 1:9 with water, prepared on the day.
  • Gloves join the other waste after the surface is wiped.
  • Washing is the final step, whether or not you wore gloves.
