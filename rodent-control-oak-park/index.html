Five safeguards for droppings and nest scraps.

Hantavirus and other germs can ride on dust from dried waste, and that is why the method below avoids anything that stirs it up.

  • Sweeping, air hoses and vacuums throw invisible grit where you breathe.
  • Attics, garages and sheds hold stale air, so let the breeze flow for half an hour before entering.
  • Choose gloves long enough to cover the wrist, where dust likes to land.
  • Spray, wait, then lift the wet mess with a towel in one motion.
  • Sponges, mops and brushes used in the cleanup count as waste as well.
  • When the job ends, lather your hands in warm water for twenty seconds or longer.
