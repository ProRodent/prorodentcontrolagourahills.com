What is known about Lake Lindero.

Published sources describe Lake Lindero as an artificial lake and neighborhood in the Santa Monica Mountains foothills, inside the city of Agoura Hills. About 120 private homes ring the lake. A few commercial buildings and a nine-hole public golf course share the area. The land was once part of a ranch, and a developer dug the lake after the property was sold in 1962.

The housing tract followed, built from the late 1960s into the early 1970s. Agoura Hills lists Lake Lindero among its first housing tracts. That is the extent of what we can verify, and this page keeps its claims to that and adds practical steps.

What a tract of that vintage can mean for a visit.

Houses from that era commonly have attic spaces reached by a hatch, wood-framed eaves and garages attached to the main structure. We do not know the details of any house here, and many may have been remodeled. The inspection looks at the seams where original framing meets later additions, because settling opens gaps.

  • Eave and soffit joints where the roof overhang meets the wall.
  • Garage door seals and the sill beneath them.
  • Pipe and cable penetrations on the exterior wall.
  • Vents with bent or missing screens.
  • Seams around additions, bump-outs and converted garages.

The sealing page explains the metal materials used to close such gaps.

Water, plantings and the last twenty feet.

A lakeside lot may have lawn, shrubs and mature trees close to the house. These are normal features, and the question is only how close. Branches that brush the roof give climbing rodents a bridge, and shrubs against the wall shelter the last stretch of a trip.

Evenings, walkers and open turf.

Open turf and paths draw people and animals at different hours. We make no claim about activity at the golf course or around the lake. For your own home, the safest plan is to keep trash lidded, pet food indoors overnight and bird feeders away from the walls.

Spot burrows, droppings or chewed items by a path or planter? Take photos and say so on the call. The yard page shows how outdoor findings are handled.

Steps for tonight.

Droppings should stay untouched, and children and pets belong elsewhere. Bait is a poor idea because poisoned animals may die inside walls, and sticky boards also catch other wildlife. If cleaning cannot wait, use the CDC box below.

Give us the room, the time and the sense that caught it, whether sound, sight or smell. Details help; stories do not.

A wet-wipe routine for pellets and nests.

Dried rodent waste breaks into fine particles, so the safe way to deal with it is slow and damp. These steps follow public health guidance and apply even if you never book a visit.

  • Health agencies tell people not to sweep or vacuum dry rodent waste, so we repeat the warning here.
  • Prop the doors and windows of a shut-up space, then give it about thirty minutes alone.
  • Skin picks up what gloves block, so wear them before reaching for stained items.
  • Let the liquid sit for around five minutes; the wait does the disinfecting.
  • Sponges, mops and brushes used in the cleanup count as waste as well.
  • Washing is the final step, whether or not you wore gloves.

Questions about Lake Lindero visits.

Is Lake Lindero its own city?

No. It is a neighborhood inside Agoura Hills, built around an artificial lake. Bookings use your street address, and the home ZIP codes for the city are 91301 and 91376.

Do you know how old my house is?

Not from the address. The tract dates from the late 1960s into the early 1970s according to published sources, but individual homes may have been remodeled or expanded. We ask and check the seams on site.

Does living by water mean more rodents?

Not automatically. Water and plant cover can draw animals, though we have no data on rodent numbers at the lake. The technician checks what touches your walls and where the cover sits.

Can you work near the golf course edge?

Yes, if your property is on private land. We make no claims about the course itself, and we ask where your lot meets it.

Is someone there tonight?

A person is on the line overnight. If tomorrow's calendar has an opening, the first caller who asks gets it.
