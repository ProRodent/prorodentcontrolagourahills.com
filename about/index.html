What you are looking at.

Phone calls about rodents in Agoura Hills, California, and in 21 surrounding places across Los Angeles and Ventura counties come to Agoura Hills Pro Rodent Control. Someone is always on the line, which counts for a lot, because rodent trouble tends to announce itself at midnight rather than at lunch.

There is no founding year, no trophy shelf and no wall of star ratings here, because none of those could be verified and so none are shown. What we can give is an honest account of how a job unfolds.

Following one job from dial tone to final check.

  • You describe the sound, the sighting or the smell.
  • You give the street address, and we offer the earliest slot we hold.
  • At the property, the technician follows the animal's trail, identifies it and puts a separate price on each stage.
  • Traps, seals, cleanup and carpentry are then sequenced so you do not pay twice for the same step.
  • A pre-set return trip inspects the traps and pushes on every seal.

Why the footer shows Ladyface Trail.

The footer carries 5614 Ladyface Trail, Agoura Hills, CA 91301 so that directories can list us. Nobody works there and it is no shop front, so skip the drive. Bookings go by the street address of your property, and the places we serve are on the areas page.

Sources for what we say about the area.

A few facts about the area appear on this site: the city's position between the Simi Hills and the Santa Monica Mountains, two landmark names and some details about Lake Lindero. Their source is an online encyclopedia plus local research, and every one comes with an action an owner or tenant can take. On health questions we defer to the CDC and the EPA, and the articles link to their pages.

How we picked the 21 places.

Coverage is organized by ZIP code. We used Agoura Hills as the origin and measured the straight-line miles to every ZIP in the coverage file, retaining the ones within about fifteen. Twenty-one places were left, from Westlake Village at just over three miles to Camarillo near fifteen.

A place remained only when two separate sources matched: the coverage data and a ZIP geocoder. Any mismatch meant removal. The areas page explains how to read the results.

What the estimate spells out.

  • The animal the inspection concluded you have.
  • Every opening found, matched to the material that shuts it.
  • Four separate prices, covering traps, sealing, cleanup and repairs.
  • The dates set for return trips.
  • Trouble noticed along the way that belongs to another trade, such as a roof leak.

You approve one line at a time. Anything unneeded never appears, and you are never pushed to accept all of it just to launch the first step.

How these pages were written.

We pictured a reader in a corridor holding a torch. Every article and page is aimed at that reader, with brief sentences and open statements about what we do not know. If only someone at the property can answer, the text admits it and sends you to the phone.

Any claim about a place that we could not check was cut. That is why street histories, building ages and shop names are absent; none of them tells you whether a gap needs sealing.

Limits of a website.

No web page can see behind drywall. Pages therefore ask what clues you hold, and a technician verifies them in person. We offer no medical advice and no guaranteed outcome ahead of an inspection.

Some problems lie outside this trade: leaking roofs, failing septic lines, rotten sill plates. A fair estimate tells you so directly.

Hours and response.

Our phone is answered by a person all day and all night. When today has an opening, that slot is yours to take; when it does not, you hear so at once.
