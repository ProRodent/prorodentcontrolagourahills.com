Read this before you clean.

Dried rodent waste breaks into fine particles, so the safe way to deal with it is slow and damp. These steps follow public health guidance and apply even if you never book a visit.

  • Sweeping, air hoses and vacuums throw invisible grit where you breathe.
  • Make a draft with two openings, then busy yourself elsewhere until the time is up.
  • Gloves while working and soap afterward make a pair that protects you.
  • Spray, wait, then lift the wet mess with a towel in one motion.
  • Sponges, mops and brushes used in the cleanup count as waste as well.
  • Rinse the sprayer and any tools, then wash yourself.
