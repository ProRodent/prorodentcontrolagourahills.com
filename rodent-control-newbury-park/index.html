A Southern California year.

A hot, dry spell sends animals looking for water, and a sudden storm sends them looking for dry cover. Neither pattern is tied to one month, which is why gaps are best closed before either arrives.

What surrounds the building.

Fences and block walls act as highways. A rat can follow the top of a fence to a roof edge without touching the ground, so fence tops are part of the survey.

Mature trees are a common feature of suburban lots in the region. A limb that reaches the roof gives climbing rodents a bridge, and the eave below is where the first sealing usually goes.
